Research

Working Papers
Innovation Dynamics and Acquisition Decisions
Second-Year Paper

Technological similarity helps determine a target's value to an acquirer, but this relationship changes as firms innovate. Using semantic embeddings of patent portfolios, I show that both current technological distance and the history of technological convergence help explain acquisition choices among otherwise similar targets.

Scheduled for presentation: FMA 2026 · SFA 2026
Work in Progress
The Evolution of Firm Disclosure
with Diego Garcia

Using Moody's Industrial Manuals from 1920--1969, we study which accounting entries firms disclosed, how disclosure differed across firms, and how reporting practices evolved over time.

Access to Finance and Local Economic Growth after World War II

Using Moody's Government Manuals, this project studies how differences in local access to finance shaped economic growth in the postwar United States.
