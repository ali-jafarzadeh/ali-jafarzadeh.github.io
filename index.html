Moody's 20th-Century Industrial Data: Variety and Quality of Firm Disclosure
This project constructs a large-scale historical dataset from Moody's twentieth-century industrial manuals to study variation in the quantity, composition, and quality of firm disclosure over time.

Outside academia, I enjoy volleyball, exploring the outdoors, Persian music, history, culture, and spending time with family and friends.
