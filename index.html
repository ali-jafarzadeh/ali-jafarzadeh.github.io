Research

Innovation Dynamics and Acquisition Decisions
Second-Year Paper

Technological similarity helps determine a target's value to an acquirer, but that relationship changes as both firms innovate. Using semantic embeddings of firms' patent portfolios, I show that both current technological distance and the history of convergence help explain acquisition choices among otherwise similar targets.

Paper
Scheduled for presentation at FMA 2026 and SFA 2026.
