Research

Working Paper
Innovation Dynamics and Acquisition Decisions
Second-Year Paper

Technological similarity helps determine a target's value to an acquirer, but this relationship changes as firms innovate. Using semantic embeddings of patent portfolios, I show that both current technological distance and the history of technological convergence help explain acquisition choices among otherwise similar targets.

About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at the Leeds School of Business at the University of Colorado Boulder.

Before joining CU Boulder, I earned an M.S. in Economics with a minor in Mathematics from Tarbiat Modares University and a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tehran.

Outside research, I enjoy volleyball, exploring the outdoors, and learning about Persian music, history, culture, and philosophy. I also value spending time with family and friends.
